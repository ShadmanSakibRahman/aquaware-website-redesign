AquaWare Watervergunningchecker Available as a service

Read water discharge permits faster. Prepare for the WFD with focus.

You supply your discharge permits. AquaWare extracts conditions, substances and emission limits, each with a reference to the source document, and flags possible points of attention for Water Framework Directive (WFD) review. No new software for your organisation: you receive the result.

Discuss your permit analysis See how it works

What you receive

From dozens of pages of permit text to targeted insight into what is needed for permit updates and WFD review.

  • Overview per permit Conditions, substances and emission limits with source references.
  • Points of attention for the WFD review Discharges and conditions that may need closer review.
  • Prioritisation Which permits deserve attention first and which data are missing.

The assessment itself stays with your expert; we provide the substantiation.
