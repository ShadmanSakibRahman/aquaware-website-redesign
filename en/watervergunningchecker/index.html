AquaWare Watervergunningchecker Available

Analyse water permits faster. Make them WFD-proof, more precisely.

The AquaWare Watervergunningchecker helps water authorities and other competent authorities analyse existing discharge permits faster, surface their discharge requirements and identify possible points for updating.

Discuss your WFD challenge See how it works

What does it deliver?

From dozens of pages of permit text to targeted insight into what's needed for permit updating and WFD assessment.

  • Less manual work Read permits automatically and collect conditions, substances and discharge limits in one clear overview.
  • Insight into WFD attention points Flag which discharges and conditions may need further assessment under the WFD.
  • Targeted updating Prioritise permits that need attention and gain insight into the next steps required.
