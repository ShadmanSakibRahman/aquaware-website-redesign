Which sources lie behind your water quality problem?

AquaWare maps the potential emission sources around your water body or monitoring point and turns them into concrete priorities for follow-up investigation.

WFD task 2028–2033

Support with the WFD pressures per water body.

For the 2028–2033 river basin management plans, water managers must substantiate the relevant pressures and impacts per water body. The Source Scan helps to identify potential sources, assess existing indications and make missing information visible.

  • 1

    Identify relevant pressures

    Which point sources and diffuse sources in the catchment are relevant to the substances that fail, classified by WFD pressure type.

  • 2

    Assess indications and uncertainties

    Per pressure: which indications there are, how strong they are and what information is still missing.

  • 3

    Prepare next steps and measures

    Targeted measurements and investigation to remove the main uncertainties, and input for targeted measures.

You assess, we substantiate. AquaWare provides research information to support your assessment of significant pressures, but does not carry out that assessment. The judgement and the submission to the Waterkwaliteitsportaal remain with you. On request we deliver the results in the table structure of the reporting request.

How AquaWare carries out the investigation

Survey broadly. Investigate selectively.

We combine data that is normally scattered, with our own analysis environment as the basis. Which source categories we can investigate depends on the substances and the available data.

  • WFD status and monitoring data
  • Companies and permits
  • Emission registers
  • WWTPs and sewerage
  • Land use and crops
  • Contaminated sites
  • Your own data

Substantiated and verifiable

We assess potential sources on the basis of available data, relevant emission routes and the strength of the indications. In doing so, we distinguish between possible and documented emissions.

The result is a traceable prioritisation for follow-up investigation, including uncertainties and missing information.

What the Source Scan does not do

  • No full quantitative source apportionment
  • No finding that a company causes a standard to be exceeded
  • No substitute for measurements, supervision or your judgement

Want to continue the analysis yourself after the scan? Our analysis environment is also available as the AquaWare Werktool.
