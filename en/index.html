From detection to source control

Which sources explain what we measure in the water?

A measurement does not tell you where a substance comes from — and whoever has thousands of companies in view does not know where to begin. AquaWare links monitoring data with companies, activities, permits and discharge routes, so that water authority, environmental agency, province and municipality get from signal to source to action together.

Formal follow-up

Your own case management system

The Werktool is not a new case system. Your existing case system stays in the lead for formal cases and files. AquaWare supports the work before and around it: selecting, analysing, prioritising, substantiating and collaborating — until it is clear which case must be taken up formally. That way you deploy your capacity as efficiently as possible up front, and then continue in your own permitting and enforcement system.

  • Sampling
  • Site visit
  • Permitting
  • Supervision
  • Enforcement

Results from investigations and supervision flow back into the company profile and the next round of prioritisation.Your case management system remains the system of record.

In practice · Schone Maaswaterketen

From hotspot analysis to lasting action at the source in the Meuse region.

Challenge
Partners in the Meuse basin want to know which companies may be discharging harmful substances. The data needed for that is scattered across many sources.
AquaWare approach
AquaWare developed the Hotspottool: data sources on sectors and potential substance discharges (SVHC and WFD) linked in one dashboard, filterable by treatment-plant area, competent authority or substance group.
Result
The tool is available to partners of the Schone Maaswaterketen, as an indicative first overview and a starting point for targeted investigation. Not proof of discharge.
