Traceability

Every choice can be traced back.

Priorities, statuses and notes are recorded per company, along with who changed what and when, so a shortlist remains well-founded even when staff change.

  • Activity and loggingChanges to status, competent authority or permit details, with user and time.
  • Notes and documentsPermits, sampling reports and notes are kept in the company file.
  • Source referencesSubstances and risk signals refer to the source used, such as the SVHC navigator or your own monitoring data.
  • Façade check via Street ViewA quick visual check of the façade and site from the company file, recorded with date and user.

The Werktool is not a new case management system.

Formal cases and files stay in your existing case management system.

AquaWare supports the work before and around it: selecting, analysing, prioritising, building the case and collaborating, until it is clear which cases should be taken up formally.
