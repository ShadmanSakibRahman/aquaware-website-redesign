Traceability

Every choice can be traced back.

Priorities, statuses and notes are recorded per company, along with who changed what and when, so a shortlist remains well-founded even when staff change.

  • Activity and loggingChanges to status, competent authority or permit details, with user and time.
  • Notes and documentsPermits, sampling reports and notes are kept in the company file.
  • Source referencesSubstances and risk signals refer to the source used, such as the national navigator for substances of very high concern (ZZS) or your own monitoring data.
  • Façade check via Street ViewA quick visual check of the façade and site from the company file, recorded with date and user.

  • Data access between organisations

    Users get access based on their organisation, role and work area. What information is shared between organisations is agreed per collaboration and governed by the applicable agreements.

  • Data quality and traceability

    Every source in the AquaWare Werktool has a provenance and an update date. Limitations of a source (coverage, currency, level of detail) are shown with the data. Every selection and priority can be traced back to the underlying source, substance and measurement, so results are reproducible and verifiable.

  • Street View and external services

    The AquaWare website makes no external requests and sets no tracking cookies. The secured Werktool is a separate system; external integrations in it, such as map or street-view services, are agreed per client and recorded in the data processing agreement.

The AquaWare Werktool is not a new permitting and enforcement system.

Your existing permitting, inspection and enforcement system remains leading for formal cases and records.

AquaWare supports the work before and around it: selecting, analysing, prioritising, substantiating and collaborating — until it is clear which case needs formal follow-up.

That way you use your capacity up front as efficiently as possible, and then continue in your own system.
