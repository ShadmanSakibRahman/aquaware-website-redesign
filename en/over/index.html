About AquaWare

Water expertise, data and software in one specialist organisation.

We help water and environmental authorities in the Netherlands and beyond to trace, prioritise and tackle sources of pollution — together with professionals in water quality, permitting, inspection and data.

See it in practice

Our ambition

Good water quality for every waterbody, everywhere.

That starts with knowing where pollution comes from. So we build tools, data and analyses that help water managers and regulators get from a measurement to a source to action — first in the Netherlands, and with the same method wherever water quality is under pressure.

What we do

From scattered information to a well-founded next step.

We develop digital solutions that turn complex water data into insights public authorities can act on: setting priorities, underpinning policy and taking measures. The AquaWare Werktool for industrial discharges is our main product, with a growing portfolio around it covering permit analysis, agricultural sources, pharmaceutical residues and tailored analysis.

What you can count on

Reliable at the core, innovative in approach.

Water engineering

Wastewater chain, wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs), permits, discharge pathways, the Water Framework Directive (WFD) and substances of very high concern (ZZS).

Data analysis

Company, substance, permit and monitoring data linked into traceable insights.

Software development

In-house development and maintenance, set up for public organisations.

Fit for the public sector

EU hosting, roles per organisation, exportable data, DPIA and the Dutch government security baseline (BIO).

Partnership

Applied in collaboration with water and environmental authorities.

We work with public bodies responsible for water quality, permitting and inspection — often in cross-agency partnerships, in which water managers, environmental and permitting authorities and local government work from one shared picture. In the Netherlands this includes provincial authorities, regional water authorities and environmental agencies; we are happy to share references in a conversation.
